# Summary

- Small-fiber neuropathy (SFN) affects thinly myelinated Aδ and unmyelinated C fibers → burning pain, allodynia, temperature sensory loss, and **autonomic** symptoms (sweating, orthostasis, GI/GU dysmotility).
- Nerve conduction studies are typically **normal** (large fibers spared); confirmation uses skin biopsy IENFD, QST, and autonomic tests (QSART, TST).
- Causes: diabetes/IGT, HIV, amyloid, Fabry, sodium-channel variants (*SCN9A/10A/11A*), autoimmune (including Sjögren), idiopathic (~half in some series).
- Thiamine deficiency is a recognized nutritional cause of neuropathy (often mixed fiber) and can present with burning extremities—relevant overlap with this patient’s documented dry beriberi.

# Key data points

- Diagnostic criteria remain heterogeneous; reduced IENFD is the most widely accepted objective marker.
- Autonomic SFN may dominate over pain in some patients (pure autonomic or mixed).
- Gain-of-function sodium-channel variants explain a subset of “idiopathic” young SFN.
- Treat causes when found; symptomatic neuropathic agents are supportive.

# Similarity to Drift0r case (0–5)

**Score:** 4

**Why:**
- **Strong matches:** Documented severe burning neuropathic pain, partial hand paralysis era, electric paresthesias, residual allodynia; low basal temperature/cold sensitivity fit small-fiber/autonomic territory; normal large-fiber–type exams possible; B1 deficiency is a **confirmed** etiology that can produce this phenotype and responded to benfotiamine.
- **Incomplete as sole disease:** SFN does **not** by itself explain severe primary osteoporosis, hypercalciuria/SLC7A9, hypogonadism, HαT, IgG4↑, imaging-neg polyarthralgia, or contested intracellular pathogens. Joint fragility is more connective-tissue/bone than pure SFN.
- **Best framing:** SFN/dysautonomia is a **high-overlap mechanism layer** (historically B1-driven, possibly residual or multi-hit) inside a multi-disease stack—not a full unifier.

# Caveats

- Post-beriberi residual SFN can persist after repletion.
- Skin biopsy timing and body site affect IENFD interpretation.
- Do not stop at “idiopathic SFN” without metabolic, endocrine, and hematologic workup.
