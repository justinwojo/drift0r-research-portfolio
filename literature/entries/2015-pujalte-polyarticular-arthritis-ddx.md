# Summary

- Practical review separating true articular pain from periarticular pain, then classifying polyarticular symptoms as noninflammatory versus inflammatory.
- Noninflammatory arthritis (chiefly osteoarthritis) lacks warm/swollen joints, typically has <1 hour morning stiffness, and worsens with activity; review of systems is usually negative for systemic inflammatory features.
- Inflammatory arthritis shows warm swollen joints, prolonged morning stiffness, and often positive systemic review; next step is pattern analysis (number, symmetry, onset) plus serology/imaging.
- Emphasizes that CRP/ESR help support the noninflammatory vs inflammatory split but are not stand-alone diagnoses.

# Key data points

- First branch: articular vs nonarticular/periarticular pain by history and exam patterns.
- Second branch: noninflammatory vs inflammatory features (warmth, swelling, morning stiffness duration, systemic ROS).
- Noninflammatory pattern: variable onset, no warm/swollen joints, pain with activity, short morning stiffness.
- Inflammatory pattern: warm/swollen joints, prolonged stiffness, systemic clues (rash, oral ulcers, organ involvement).
- Pattern of joint involvement (symmetry, additive vs migratory, small vs large joints) narrows etiology after inflammation is classified.

# Similarity to Drift0r case (0–5)

**Score:** 4

**Why:** Case is multi-year migratory→generalized polyarthralgia without swelling/heat/discoloration and with repeatedly normal CRP/ESR—exactly the noninflammatory branch this paper prioritizes. Framework is highly applicable; paper itself is general OA-heavy and does not address joint fragility, osteoporosis, or multi-system disease stack.

# Caveats

- Written for primary care; not a deep specialty differential for seronegative or neuropathic mimics.
- “Noninflammatory” here often maps to OA; Drift0r’s age, migratory course, and multi-system findings are not classic OA.
- Does not cover hypermobility spectrum, small-fiber neuropathy, endocrine arthropathy, or infection-associated arthralgia in depth.
- Not medical advice; educational research note only.
