# Summary

- Case of a female veterinarian with predominantly rheumatologic symptoms and documented dual bacteremia (B. koehlerae and B. henselae) by serology, enrichment blood culture, PCR, and sequencing.
- Long-term antibiotic management associated with infection clearance and symptom resolution including improvement in acquired joint hypermobility resembling EDS type III.
- Authors propose systemic bartonellosis as a differential in incalcitrant rheumatologic disease with high arthropod/animal exposure.

# Key data points

- Dual species infection confirmed with multi-modal methods, not serology alone.
- Clinical phenotype dominated by rheumatologic complaints rather than classic CSD lymphadenopathy.
- Reported substantial improvement in joint hypermobility with treatment—hypothesis-generating only.
- Occupational animal exposure is a major risk context (veterinary).
