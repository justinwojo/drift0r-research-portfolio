# Summary

- One-day clinic investigation in Ontario, Canada: 19 participants tested; **2/19** had *Babesia odocoilei* by PCR + 18S rRNA sequencing nearly identical to type strains.
- Both positives had hallmark babesiosis-type symptoms: **night sweats**, chills, fevers, profound fatigue.
- Dataset indicates ***B. odocoilei* may serologically cross-react with *B. duncani***.
- Authors argue clinicians must recognize **more than two Babesia species** cause human disease in North America; first report framing *B. odocoilei* as human pathogen in this series.

# Key data points

- Method: PCR + sequencing of 18S rRNA gene (not FISH).
- Clinical pattern emphasizes night sweats/chills/fever/fatigue—symptoms often cited in chronic TBD communities as “babesia-like.”
- Cross-reactivity warning: positive *B. duncani* serology might reflect *B. odocoilei* (or related) exposure in some patients.
- Small convenience sample; requires independent replication.
- Implications for panels limited to microti ± duncani PCR only.

# Caveats

- n=2 molecular positives; not a large controlled diagnostic study.
- Does not use or validate FISH.
- Causality for chronic illness beyond the reported acute/subacute symptom set not established.
- Needs independent labs to reproduce human *B. odocoilei* pathogenicity claims.
