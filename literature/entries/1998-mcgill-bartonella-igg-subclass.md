# Summary

- Used Western blotting to map Ig isotype and IgG subclass reactivity of CSD patient sera against B. henselae whole-cell antigen.
- Heterogeneous IgG banding with a conserved ~83 kDa immunoreactive protein (Bh83) recognized by all CSD sera tested.
- Prominent antigen recognition limited to IgG1 subclass; IgM and IgE did not react significantly on this blot system; IgA patterns resembled IgG/IgG1.
- Bh83 showed limited cross-recognition patterns useful for distinguishing some related pathogens; cross-reaction with B. quintana-infected sera observed for Bh83.

# Key data points

- Induced humoral response in CSD predominantly IgG1-directed in this analysis.
- Conserved Bh83 antigen recognized across all tested CSD sera.
- No evidence here that B. henselae infection produces an IgG4-dominant subclass profile.
- Provides immunologic baseline against which Drift0r’s IgG4↑/IgG1↓ pattern can be compared.

# Caveats

- CSD sera, not chronic multi-system or IgG4-RD-overlap phenotypes.
- Western blot of whole-cell antigen ≠ quantitative serum IgG subclass concentrations (patient has quantitative IgG4/IgG1 abnormalities).
- Does not measure total IgG4 levels during infection.
- 1998 methods; modern subclass biology of chronic infection unaddressed.
