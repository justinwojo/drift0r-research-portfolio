# Summary

- Hypophosphatasia (HPP) is defective mineralization from reduced tissue-nonspecific alkaline phosphatase (TNSALP) activity due to *ALPL* pathogenic variants.
- Spectrum spans perinatal lethal disease to adult osteomalacia/stress fractures/pseudofractures, premature tooth loss, and isolated odontohypophosphatasia.
- **Persistently low age-/sex-adjusted ALP** is central; substrates (PLP/B6, PEA, inorganic pyrophosphate pathways) and genetics confirm.
- Targeted therapy: **asfotase alfa** ERT in selected patients; supportive dental/ortho/pain care across phenotypes.

# Key data points

- Adult HPP: osteomalacia, lower-extremity stress/pseudofractures, bone pain, early tooth loss, reduced QoL; can be mislabeled “osteoporosis.”
- Inheritance: severe perinatal/infantile usually AR; milder adult/odonto forms AR or AD depending on variant effect.
- **Avoid bisphosphonates, denosumab, and excess vitamin D** in true HPP (worsen mineralization defect or confound).
- Molecular diagnosis: major/minor clinical criteria plus biallelic LOF *ALPL* or heterozygous dominant-negative *ALPL*.

# Similarity to Drift0r case (0–5)

**Score:** 2

**Why:**
- Classic **don’t-miss** metabolic bone mimic for young-adult severe low BMD and chronic MSK pain.
- **Decisive gate:** serial ALP. If ALP has been repeatedly normal/high-normal, classic HPP becomes unlikely (chart-mine all historical ALP values).
- Even if ALP borderline, HPP does not explain HαT, *SLC7A9* hypercalciuria/cystine transport genetics, thiamine-responsive neuropathy, or IgG4 lab pattern as core features.
- Research action: if any low ALP values exist → PLP/B6, PEA, *ALPL* sequencing discussion with metabolic bone specialist.

# Caveats

- Low ALP has many non-HPP causes (antiresorptives, hypothyroidism, malnutrition, Zn/Mg deficiency, massive transfusion, etc.).
- “Normal ALP HPP” is exceptional and should not override serial biochemistry without genetics/expert review.
