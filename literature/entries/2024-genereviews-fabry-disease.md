# Summary

- Fabry disease is X-linked lysosomal storage disease from *GLA* deficiency (α-galactosidase A), causing globotriaosylceramide accumulation.
- Classic male phenotype: childhood **acroparesthesias** (burning limb pain), angiokeratomas, hypohidrosis/anhidrosis, heat and exercise intolerance, GI symptoms, later CKD, hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, stroke.
- Late-onset variants may present with predominantly cardiac or renal disease and milder early pain history.
- Diagnosis: enzyme activity (males) + *GLA* sequencing; biomarkers (lyso-Gb3); family cascade testing. Disease-modifying options include enzyme replacement and chaperone therapy (selected amenable variants).

# Key data points

- Neuropathic pain is typically **distal extremity burning**, crisis-like, often provoked by **fever, heat, and physical exertion**—linked to small-fiber involvement and hypohidrosis.
- **Heat intolerance / reduced sweating** is a hallmark clue distinguishing Fabry pain from many other small-fiber neuropathies.
- Multisystem progressive storage disease—not a primary osteoporosis syndrome (secondary bone issues may occur with CKD/immobility but are not defining).
- Females can be symptomatic due to skewed X-inactivation; do not dismiss based on sex alone.

# Similarity to Drift0r case (0–5)

**Score:** 1

**Why:**
- Neuropathic pain and multi-system rare-disease framing put Fabry on long-tail DDx lists.
- **Heat mismatch note:** classic Fabry pain is heat/**fever**/exercise-triggered with hypohidrosis. If the patient’s pain pattern is cold-triggered, heat-preferring, or lacks hypohidrosis/angiokeratoma/early acroparesthesia history, similarity stays low.
- No core match to HαT genetics, *SLC7A9*, or young-male primary osteoporosis narrative.
- Enzyme/*GLA* testing only if clinical pretest (skin, sweating, family renal/cardiac disease, childhood pain crises) rises.

# Caveats

- Do not equate all small-fiber neuropathy with Fabry.
- Late-onset cardiac variants can lack childhood pain—different pretest pathway.
- Not medical advice.
