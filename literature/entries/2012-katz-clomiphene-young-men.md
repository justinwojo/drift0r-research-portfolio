# Summary

- Prospective evaluation of long-term clomiphene citrate in **young** hypogonadal men (mean age 29, range 22–37) wanting to avoid exogenous T dependency and preserve fertility potential.
- CC started 25 mg every other day, titrated to 50 mg EOD targeting T ~550 ± 50 ng/dL.
- All mean T and gonadotropin levels rose; most ADAM domains improved subjectively; ~10% non-responders reported in related summaries of this cohort.
- Argues CC is a safe, efficacious alternative to testosterone supplementation in young HG.

# Key data points

- n = 86; hypogonadism = two consecutive morning T <300 ng/dL.
- Mean treatment duration 19 ± 14 months.
- At last eval: 70% on 25 mg EOD; 30% on 50 mg EOD.
- Infertility was the most common reason for seeking care (64%).
- Free T, SHBG, estradiol, LH, FSH tracked with total T.

# Caveats

- Not RCT; fertility outcomes not the primary hard endpoint here.
- Subjective ADAM improvements; no multi-system non-gonadal outcomes.
- Gynecomastia/E2 rises may require adjunct AI — not fully characterized.
