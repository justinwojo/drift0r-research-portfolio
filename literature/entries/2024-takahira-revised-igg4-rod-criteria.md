# Summary

- Updates the 2014/2015 Japanese diagnostic criteria for IgG4-related ophthalmic disease after accumulating clinical experience.
- Retains focus on lacrimal gland, trigeminal nerve, and extraocular muscle lesions; strengthens attention to **optic neuropathy** and vision-threatening orbital disease.
- Continues multi-domain structure: imaging enlargement, histopathology with IgG4+ plasma cells, and serum IgG4 elevation.

# Key data points

- Original criteria pillars: (1) imaging enlargement of lacrimal/trigeminal/EOM or related masses; (2) histology with IgG4+/IgG+ ≥40% or >50 IgG4+ cells/HPF; (3) serum IgG4 >135 mg/dL.
- 2023 revision prioritizes recognition of optic nerve involvement causing visual acuity/field loss as a critical clinical feature for criteria/practice.
- Classic phenotype remains bilateral or progressive lacrimal/orbital inflammatory disease (Mikulicz-type spectrum), often with extraorbital IgG4-RD.
- Transient, spontaneously resolving unilateral swelling without persistent imaging lesions is outside the intended diagnostic use case.

# Caveats

- Ophthalmic criteria are organ-specific adjuncts to systemic IgG4-RD frameworks.
- Research only; not treatment guidance.
