# Summary

- IDSA evidence-based guidelines for diagnosis and management of **histoplasmosis**, updating the 2000 document.
- Covers acute pulmonary, chronic cavitary pulmonary, mediastinal, and **progressive disseminated histoplasmosis (PDH)**.
- Defines who needs therapy vs observation and outlines antifungal choices/durations by syndrome and host immune status.

# Key data points

- *Histoplasma capsulatum* is endemic in Ohio/Mississippi river valleys and other foci; Texas includes endemic regions.
- PDH: fever, weight loss, cytopenias, hepatosplenomegaly, mucosal lesions; high yield from antigen testing, blood/bone marrow culture in immunocompromised hosts.
- Chronic cavitary disease resembles TB in patients with underlying COPD/emphysema.
- Bone/joint histoplasmosis is uncommon relative to pulmonary/disseminated visceral disease but appears in disseminated cases.

# Caveats

- 2007 guideline—newer diagnostics (improved antigen, PCR) and practice nuances have accrued.
- Not a bone-disease guideline.
- Not medical advice.
