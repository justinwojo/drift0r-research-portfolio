# Why this card exists

Second of three cards filed in v0.4.0 against H3's previously empty contradicting list.

# What it addresses

This cohort is closer to the record than most of the hypercalciuria literature — men,
stone disease, idiopathic hypercalciuria — which is exactly why it is useful as a check
rather than as support. The question it speaks to is whether the 24-hour urine calcium
figure predicts *how severe* the bone loss will be, which is the specific inference the
working model needs.

# Scope limits (read before citing)

- Closer demographic overlap raises relevance; it does not make the cohort this person.
- No numeric result is carried into any claim or public page.
- The card is filed as contradicting because it bears on a step H3 assumes, not because
  it studied this case or reached a conclusion about it.
