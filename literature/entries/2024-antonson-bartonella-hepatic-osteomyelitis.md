# Summary

- Case of disseminated B. henselae with hepatic abscesses and associated ischial osteomyelitis in an immunocompetent 2-year-old.
- Emphasizes considering Bartonella when MSK complaints accompany hepatosplenic involvement.
- Molecular diagnostics central to confirmation in culture-negative disease.

# Key data points

- Immunocompetent toddler with hepatic abscesses + osteomyelitis.
- Disseminated CSD pattern rather than isolated lymphadenopathy.
- Reinforces imaging of liver/spleen when atypical CSD suspected.

# Similarity to Drift0r case (0–5)

**Score:** 1

**Why:** Confirms bone+visceral Bartonella is real but demographically and clinically distant from adult chronic polyarthralgia without hepatic abscess phenotype.

# Caveats

- Age 2 years; acute disseminated illness.
- Patient lacks described hepatic abscesses/FUO osteomyelitis package.
- Normal inflammatory markers pattern in Drift0r argues against active multifocal pyogenic-like osteomyelitis.
