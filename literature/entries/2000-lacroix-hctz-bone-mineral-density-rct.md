# Summary

- Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled 3-year trial of HCTZ 12.5 mg/day, HCTZ 25 mg/day, or placebo in 320 healthy normotensive adults aged 60–79 (205 women, 115 men).
- Primary endpoint: DXA BMD change at total hip, PA spine, and total body.
- Intention-to-treat: modest hip BMD benefit vs placebo at 36 months (~0.8–0.9 percentage points); effects stronger in women than men.
- Supports a causal BMD-preserving effect of low-dose thiazides, complementing observational hip-fracture associations.

# Key data points

- 309/320 (97%) completed 36-month visit; adherence generally high except men on 25 mg (60.5%).
- Total hip BMD difference vs placebo at 36 months: +0.79 pp (12.5 mg; 95% CI −0.12 to 1.71) and +0.92 pp (25 mg; CI −0.001 to 1.85); overall P=0.03.
- Spine benefit more evident early (6 months) for 25 mg; 36-month spine difference not statistically significant.
- No significant total-body BMD differences between groups.
- Incident falls/fractures collected but trial not powered as a fracture endpoint study.

# Caveats

- Population was older normotensive adults, not young men with secondary osteoporosis and SLC7A9 heterozygosity.
- Male subgroup effects weaker/less adherent.
- BMD deltas are modest; fracture benefit inferred partly from other literature (see Aung Cochrane).
- Must balance with NOSTONE (2023) null modern stone-recurrence findings at commonly used doses.
- Research catalog only.
