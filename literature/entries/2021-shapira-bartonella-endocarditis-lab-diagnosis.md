# Summary

- Developed EIA and multiplex real-time PCR (genus + B. henselae + B. quintana probes) for Bartonella endocarditis diagnosis; evaluated on 37 definite cases (18 B. henselae, 18 B. quintana, 1 B. koehlerae).
- Anti-Bartonella IgG titers ≥1:800 found in 94% by EIA, similar to IFA performance; species-level serodiagnosis limited by cross-reactivity.
- Cross-reactivity notes: frequent low-titer reactions with C. burnetii antibodies (Q fever endocarditis) and occasional low-titer reactivity in brucellosis/culture-positive endocarditis (especially E. faecalis).
- Valve tissue real-time PCR matched sequence-based diagnosis completely; genus probe also detected additional endocarditis-associated species at genus level.

# Key data points

- High-titer IgG (≥1:800) is the serologic sweet spot for endocarditis—not low-positive IgM alone.
- Blood/serology cross-reactivity can mislead without clinical endocarditis context.
- Tissue PCR is highly accurate when valve tissue available.
- Reinforces culture-negative endocarditis laboratory algorithm.

# Caveats

- Endocarditis-only validation population.
- Does not assess IGeneX immunoblots or FISH assays.
- High-titer IgG paradigm may not transfer to chronic MSK suspicion without endovascular infection.
