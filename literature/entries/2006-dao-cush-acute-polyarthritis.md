# Summary

- Structured approach to acute polyarthritis and polyarthralgia, spanning inflammatory rheumatic, crystal, infectious, and postinfectious causes.
- Notes that polyarthralgia without swelling can be seen in fibromyalgia, benign joint hypermobility, and many viral arthritides.
- Migratory patterns raise viral (e.g., hepatitis B–associated), gonococcal, and acute rheumatic fever considerations among others.
- Stresses time course, extra-articular clues, and early infection workup when presentation is acute or shifting.

# Key data points

- Acute multi-joint pain requires simultaneous consideration of infection, crystal disease, and early inflammatory arthritis.
- Viral arthritis/arthralgia frequently self-limited but may present with migratory or additive polyarticular pain.
- Absence of joint swelling does not exclude significant systemic drivers (viral, hypermobility, fibromyalgia-spectrum pain).
- Pattern recognition (migratory vs additive; mono→poly evolution) is diagnostically useful early.
