# Summary

- 2020 revision of Japan’s comprehensive diagnostic criteria for IgG4-related disease (three domains).
- Domains: (1) clinical and radiologic features of characteristic diffuse/localized swelling or masses in single or multiple organs; (2) serological diagnosis — serum IgG4 >135 mg/dL; (3) pathologic diagnosis — marked lymphoplasmacytic infiltrate and fibrosis with IgG4+ plasma cell criteria.
- Diagnostic certainty tiers: **definite** (1+2+3), **probable** (1+3), **possible** (1+2).
- Complements ACR/EULAR classification criteria; useful bedside framing when pathology is unavailable (“possible” at most if only serum + soft clinical features).

# Key data points

- Serum IgG4 cutoff **>135 mg/dL** (~135 mg/dL = 1.35 g/L) for the serologic domain—patient’s ~163 mg/dL meets serology alone.
- Without organ imaging/pathology meeting domain 1 and 3, diagnosis cannot reach definite/probable.
- Emphasizes exclusion of mimics (malignancy, Sjögren, Castleman, ANCA, etc.).
- Organ-specific criteria (pancreas, kidney, etc.) still refine diagnosis when available.
- Designed for clinical diagnosis pathways; ACR/EULAR better for research classification purity.

# Caveats

- “Possible IgG4-RD” is a weak category with low positive predictive value.
- Serum IgG4 rises in many non-IgG4-RD conditions (allergy, infection, vasculitis, malignancy).
- Pair with ACR/EULAR 2019 criteria (lit-0222) for dual-framework analysis.
