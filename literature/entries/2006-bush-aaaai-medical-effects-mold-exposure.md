# Summary

- AAAAI position paper reviewing proven and hypothesized human health effects of mold exposure.
- Established mechanisms: IgE-mediated allergy (asthma, rhinitis), allergic bronchopulmonary aspergillosis, hypersensitivity pneumonitis, infection in immunocompromised hosts, and irritant effects.
- Explicitly frames many newer “toxic mold” / mycotoxin multisystem illness claims as **largely or completely unproved** at the time of writing.
- Guides evaluation of patients and environments away from nonvalidated commercial mycotoxin panels toward standard allergic/occupational workups.

# Key data points

- Distinguishes well-defined allergic/infectious mold disease from speculative systemic mycotoxicosis from indoor exposure.
- Emphasizes dose, species, host immune status, and exposure context.
- Advises allergists/immunologists facing high community concern to ground care in evidence-based diagnoses.
- Triggered extensive commentary/debate in subsequent JACI correspondence—marker of controversy intensity.

# Caveats

- 2006—precedes some later dampness epidemiology and still-contested CIRS literature; read alongside Sauni/Fisk and advocacy papers.
- Position statements can reflect specialty priors; not a systematic review of all damp-building RCTs.
- Research catalog only—not advice to ignore real water damage or allergic disease.
