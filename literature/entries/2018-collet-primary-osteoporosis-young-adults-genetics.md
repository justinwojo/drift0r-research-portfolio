# Summary

- Next-generation sequencing of candidate bone genes in **123 young or middle-aged adults** labeled idiopathic osteoporosis after secondary-cause exclusions.
- **Inclusion (abstract/methods):** Z-score < −2.0 SD, diagnosis before age 55 years, **with fracture or not** — fragility fracture was **not** a required inclusion criterion.
- **Exclusion (methods):** secondary causes including malignant disease, malabsorption, **hypogonadism**, hemochromatosis, hyperthyroidism, hypercortisolism, vitamin D deficiency, mastocytosis, and bone-interfering medications.
- Reports several rare/novel variants and a high prevalence of *LRP5* p.Val667Met and other *LRP5* variants; clinical phenotype of carriers was indistinguishable within the idiopathic cohort.
- Useful for gene-list rationale in early-onset/idiopathic OP workups **after** secondary evaluation — not as a yield estimate for patients who still have untreated or historical secondary diagnoses.

# Key data points (yield figures — keep numerators explicit)

- Cohort N = **123**.
- **11/123 (≈8.9%)** carried rare or novel variants in *COL1A2* (4), *PLS3* (2), *WNT1* (4), or *DKK1* (1).
- **22/123 (17.8%)** carried *LRP5* p.Val667Met (including 3 homozygotes).
- **16/123 (≈13%)** carried a novel or very rare *LRP5* variant (separate statement from Val667Met prevalence).
- Public language must not collapse these into a single “gene panel yield ≈10–15%” without specifying which numerator/denominator and variant class.
- Functional assays: reported *LRP5* missense variants reduced canonical WNT luciferase activity.

# Similarity to Drift0r case (0–5)

**Score:** 2 (revised 2026-08 M4R)

**Why revised downward:**
- Age and low BMD remain directionally relevant.
- **Hypogonadism was an exclusion** in Collet et al.; this case’s historical hypogonadism (and other secondaries) means the patient would **not** have entered this “idiopathic” sequencing cohort as defined.
- Fracture was optional in Collet; do not claim the study required fractures matching any particular skeletal event set.
- Monogenic co-drivers can still be a research question, but this paper’s yield **does not transfer** as a prior for multi-secondary phenotypes.

# Caveats

- Do not cite this card to support “high gene-panel yield in this phenotype” without the explicit subset math above.
- Secondary causes must still be treated when found; genetics does not replace hypercalciuria management or endocrine care.
- Do not invent a cohort VUS rate from this paper: Collet labels *COL1A2* p.(Pro471Leu) as a variant of unknown significance (VUS) in **one** patient only; no VUS rate or denominator is reported.
- Identifier identity was verified; summary accuracy is a separate judgment.
