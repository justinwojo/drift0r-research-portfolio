# Summary

- **ESCEO 2024 evidence-based guideline** specifically for osteoporosis in men—first major international male-focused GRADE guideline package after long reliance on the 2012 Endocrine Society CPG (Watts) and female-dominant literature.
- Covers disease burden in men, densitometric interpretation (including use of a **female reference database** for densitometric diagnosis in men as discussed by the group), absolute fracture risk, treatment thresholds, pharmacologic options, monitoring, and health-economic considerations.
- Explicitly addresses that male osteoporosis remains under-recognized despite substantial fracture morbidity/mortality.
- Future research gap called out: efficacy data for denosumab and bone-forming agents specifically in men remain thinner than postmenopausal literature.

# Key data points

- Working group: European Society for Clinical and Economic Aspects of Osteoporosis, Osteoarthritis and Musculoskeletal Diseases (ESCEO) international multidisciplinary panel.
- Methods: comprehensive evidence review + GRADE-assessed recommendations (diagnosis, monitoring, treatment).
- Emphasizes fracture-risk approach rather than T-score alone; secondary-cause evaluation remains foundational in men.
- Pharmacologic first-line framing in companion summaries: oral bisphosphonates (alendronate/risedronate) for many high-risk men; other agents per risk and prior fracture context.
- Positions male osteoporosis as a full clinical entity, not a rare footnote to postmenopausal disease.

# Caveats

- Abstract-level access for this card; verify full recommendation text before clinical citation of specific grade strengths.
- Young men with extreme secondary osteoporosis may fall outside trial populations underpinning drug recommendations.
- Does not adjudicate infection, HαT, thiamine, or IgG4 patterns as bone mechanisms.
- Not medical advice; Endocrine Society 2012 remains historically cited in U.S. practice.
