# Summary

- Systematic review and meta-analysis of cannabis use vs semen quality and reproductive hormones (T, FSH, LH).
- Across pooled studies, cannabis users did **not** show a statistically significant overall excess of abnormal semen parameters or lower testosterone vs non-users.
- Small significant differences in gonadotropins in some analyses (lower FSH/LH SMD) but authors conclude **no clear clinically significant association** with testicular function; residual confounding and study quality limit certainty.
- Useful as a reality check against over-attributing profound hypogonadism solely to cannabis.

# Key data points

- 9 studies; semen data n ≈ 4014; hormone data n ≈ 4787.
- Any abnormal semen parameter RR 1.16 (95% CI 0.84–1.60; p = 0.37).
- Testosterone SMD −0.14 (95% CI −0.41 to 0.13; p = 0.32).
- FSH SMD −0.14 (p = 0.005); LH SMD more negative in abstract estimates — interpret cautiously amid heterogeneity.

**Why:**

# Caveats

- Observational; exposure misclassification (THC/CBD, chronicity, recent use).
- Not a trial of cessation for hypogonadism recovery.
- Does not address multi-system non-reproductive illness.
