# Summary

- Experimental physiology RCT in healthy men 20–50: goserelin suppressed endogenous gonadal steroids; graded testosterone gel repletion ± **anastrozole** to block aromatization to estradiol.
- Disentangles androgen vs estrogen contributions: androgen deficiency → lean mass/muscle/strength loss; estrogen deficiency → fat gain; **both** contribute to sexual function decline.
- Foundational for understanding why over-suppressing estradiol with AIs can harm body composition and sexual function even if T looks “high.”

# Key data points

- ~198 men in T-dose arms without AI; ~202 with anastrozole co-administration.
- Without AI, body fat rose at very low T (including placebo gel mean T ~44 ng/dL — order of magnitude similar to patient’s ~34).
- Lean mass/thigh muscle fell at lowest T doses; sexual desire fell as T dose reduced.
- With anastrozole, estrogen deficiency effects emerge despite T repletion.

**Why:**

# Caveats

- Healthy volunteers under experimental suppression — not idiopathic disease.
- Short (16 weeks); not a treatment guideline for AI co-therapy dosing.
- Does not validate weekly 1 mg anastrozole protocols used clinically off-label.
