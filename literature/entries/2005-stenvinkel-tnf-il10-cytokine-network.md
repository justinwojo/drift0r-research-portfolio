# Summary

- Reviews interdependent roles of pro-inflammatory TNF-α / IL-6 and anti-inflammatory IL-10 in **uremic / CKD** inflammatory states.
- Provides immunobiology context for cytokine networks in dialysis-associated chronic inflammation.
- **Does not** establish diagnostic rules, cutoffs, or predicted mild cytokine patterns for non-renal multi-system illness.

# Key data points

- Scope is uremia/CKD cytokine network physiology and inflammation associated with kidney failure.
- TNF-α and IL-10 are discussed as central, interacting mediators in that renal setting.
- Assay timing, platform, and intercurrent illness affect measured cytokine levels in general — but this card does **not** re-derive a general single-timepoint rule for non-CKD patients from this paper alone.
