# Why this card exists

H2 — the working model in which an inherited bone-fragility condition explains the
readings — also carried an **empty contradicting-literature list**. This is one of two
cards filed in v0.4.0 to close that gap.

# What it addresses

What a bone-fragility gene panel actually finds in people referred for unexplained
early-onset osteoporosis. The reason this counts *against* a genetic explanation rather
than for one is the shape of the yield: most people referred do not get an answer from
the panel. Presentation alone therefore does not establish a genetic cause.

# Scope limits (read before citing)

- **This cuts both ways and the card must not be used one-way.** Partial yield means a
  genetic cause cannot be assumed — and equally, a negative panel would not rule one out.
  The record currently contains **no panel result at all** (see UQ-0013), so neither
  direction is available here.
- Cohort is children and young adults at a referral centre; referral populations are
  selected, and yield figures do not transfer directly to any individual.
- Complements the existing card lit-0294 rather than duplicating it.
- Open access, but no numeric yield figure is carried into any claim or public page.
