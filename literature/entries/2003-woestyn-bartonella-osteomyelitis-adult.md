# Summary

- First description (at publication) of osteomyelitis due to B. henselae genotype I in an immunocompetent middle-aged woman.
- Diagnosis established by serology, histopathology, and PCR of osseous and lymph node tissues.
- Illustrates that culture-negative osteomyelitis workups should consider Bartonella when epidemiology/histology fit, even in immunocompetent adults.

# Key data points

- Immunocompetent adult—not classic pediatric multifocal CSD osteomyelitis only.
- Tissue PCR critical; blood culture alone insufficient historically for Bartonella osteomyelitis.
- Genotype I identified.
- Supports multi-modal confirmation (serology + tissue molecular + pathology).

**Score:** 3

**Why:** Adult immunocompetent bone infection is in the same broad anatomic domain as Drift0r’s mild humeral periosteal edema, though that MRI finding is far milder than frank osteomyelitis and lacks tissue confirmation.

# Caveats

- Single case of frank osteomyelitis vs mild periosteal edema without mass/abscess.
- No forearm nodule correlation.
- Requires tissue for definitive diagnosis—unavailable/not obtained in patient for this indication.
- Does not validate serology-only chronic disease labels.
