# Summary

- Practical adult SM update covering diagnosis (major/minor criteria), subtype classification (ISM/SSM vs advanced SM), molecular risk (e.g., *ASXL1*, *RUNX1*, *SRSF2*), and management.
- **ISM treatment goals**: anaphylaxis prevention, symptom control, **osteoporosis treatment**.
- Reiterates that diagnosis centers on **bone marrow/tissue pathology** plus KIT and immunophenotype, not tryptase alone.
- Notes that in known HαT, tryptase interpretation for the >20 minor criterion should be **adjusted** (aligned with WHO/ICC discussion).

# Key data points

- Major: multifocal dense MC infiltrates (≥15) in BM/extracutaneous organs.
- Minor: morphology, KIT activating mutation, aberrant CD markers, tryptase >20 (with HαT/AMN caveats).
- Osteoporosis/fractures are clinically important in indolent disease and warrant proactive bone care if SM is confirmed.

# Similarity to Drift0r case (0–5)

**Score:** 3

**Why:** Framework paper for “could this still be SM?” Patient fails blood-based minor criteria package (tryptase <20, KIT neg, no MCAS), so pre-test probability is low—but **markedly low BMD for age** is exactly the sort of end-organ clue that keeps SM on a “don’t miss” list and makes deferred marrow a conscious risk tradeoff (BMD procedure risk vs diagnostic yield).

# Caveats

- Written for hematology practice populations, not primary HαT clinics.
- Does not quantify SM prevalence among HαT+ men with isolated markedly low BMD for age and normal mediators.
