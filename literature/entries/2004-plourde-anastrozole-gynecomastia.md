# Summary

- Double-blind RCT of anastrozole 1 mg daily vs placebo for 6 months in boys 11–18 with persistent pubertal gynecomastia.
- Anastrozole raised T/E2 ratio as expected but **did not significantly increase the rate of ≥50% breast volume reduction** vs placebo (38.5% vs 31.4%).
- Well tolerated; hormonal shifts confirm aromatase blockade.
- Important negative lesson: lowering estradiol pharmacologically does not reliably reverse established gynecomastia tissue.

# Key data points

- n = 80 randomized.
- Response definition: ≥50% reduction in total breast volume by ultrasound.
- Odds ratio for response ~1.51 (95% CI 0.50–4.84; p = 0.47) — not significant.
- Median % change in T/E2 ratio: +166% (anastrozole) vs +39% (placebo).
