# Summary

- Systematic assessment of **BMD, bone turnover markers, and fractures** in **82 adults with indolent systemic mastocytosis (ISM)**.
- WHO osteoporosis (T-score < −2.5) in **~20%**; mastocytosis-related low BMD (Z-score < −2) more frequent in **men (28%)** than women (9%).
- Spine BMD generally worse than hip; **vertebral fractures** common (including in some patients without very low Z-scores).
- **No significant correlation** between serum tryptase and BMD T/Z-scores in this cohort.

# Key data points

- Mean age ~48 years—younger than typical primary osteoporosis populations.
- Bone turnover markers (bALP, CTX, osteocalcin) used to profile remodeling.
- Fragility fractures, especially vertebral, are a major clinical burden in ISM.

# Caveats

- All participants already had ISM; cannot estimate how often isolated osteoporosis is SM.
- Tryptase–BMD non-correlation means normal/modest tryptase does not fully exclude bone-relevant mast cell disease, but also means high tryptase does not grade bone severity.
