# Launch FAQ — prepared public responses

> **Research only — not medical advice.** Short answers maintainers can link or paste. Not a substitute for LEGAL.md.

## 1. “AI cannot diagnose”

**Response:** Correct. This project **does not diagnose**. It is an AI-**assisted** research packaging of public case materials and open literature. Multi-model agreement is disclosed as research method — **not** clinical validation. Licensed clinicians must verify primary records and literature before any clinical use. Do not start, stop, or change treatment based on this site.

## 2. Specialty-lab / LDT / “pseudoscience” concerns

**Response:** Contested specialty laboratory-developed tests (for example some Babesia FISH or Bartonella antibody signals) are presented as **contested research signals**, not as established infection diagnoses. Where commercial/PCR pathways disagree or confirmation is missing, the portfolio says so. Prefer guideline-level sources and counterevidence. Critiques with citable literature are welcome via the **Contradictory evidence** issue template. This is not a defense of any commercial lab product.

## 3. Privacy / consent concerns

**Response:** Drift0r granted broad permission to publish this research portfolio (2026-08-05). **Permission is not endorsement** of findings. Original source PDFs are not redistributed by this repository or static site; a patient-hosted external folder may exist under Drift0r’s control and can change.  

If you believe published text exceeds consent, is inaccurate, or should be limited:

1. Open a **Correction, privacy, or removal request** issue.  
2. **Do not post private medical records** in the public issue.  
3. Sensitive consent or **withdrawal** from Drift0r may continue through existing private correspondence with the repository owner.  

There is **no published privacy@ email**. Public issues are public.

## 5. Why is there no clinician review?

**Response:** Clinician review has **not been performed** and that status is stated prominently. The owner accepted publishing a **research preview** before clinician review solely because the absence of review is unambiguous and cannot be mistaken for endorsement. **Scoped licensed clinician review remains the highest-priority post-launch task.** Until then, treat all rankings and interpretations as unaudited research.

## 6. Can I get personal medical advice here?

**No.** Issues that request personal advice, dosing, or treatment plans will be closed. See a licensed clinician who can access your records.

## 7. How do I contribute research?

Use structured GitHub issue templates only (literature, citation fix, counterevidence, alternative hypothesis). Prefer DOI/PMID. No unsolicited dumps of private records. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## 8. Will removing something from the site erase it everywhere?

**No guarantee.** Official surfaces can be corrected, redacted, or unpublished. Forks, clones, archives, and caches may persist. See [COMMUNITY_MODERATION.md](COMMUNITY_MODERATION.md).
