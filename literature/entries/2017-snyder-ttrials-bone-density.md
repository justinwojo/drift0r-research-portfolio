# Summary

- Placebo-controlled RCT (Testosterone Trials Bone Trial): 1 year of testosterone gel vs placebo in men ≥65 with low T.
- Primary mechanistic bone outcomes via QCT volumetric BMD and finite-element estimated strength.
- T treatment significantly increased spine and hip vBMD and estimated strength — larger effects in trabecular than peripheral bone and larger at spine than hip.
- Supports hypogonadism/low T as a reversible contributor to low bone strength; does **not** prove fracture reduction (see TRAVERSE 2024).

# Key data points

- n = 211 analyzed; mean age ~72; mean BMI ~31.
- Eligibility: two T values averaging <275 ng/dL (trial criterion).
- Spine trabecular vBMD: +7.5% (T) vs +0.8% (placebo); treatment effect **+6.8%** (95% CI 4.8–8.7; p < 0.001).
- Spine trabecular estimated strength treatment effect **+8.5%** (95% CI 6.0–10.9; p < 0.001).
- aBMD by DXA also assessed; QCT effects more pronounced.

# Caveats

- Older men; short duration (1 year); not a fracture endpoint trial.
- Patient also has hypercalciuria, thiamine deficiency history, possible inflammatory/infectious drivers — hypogonadism is one of several bone insults.
- Units/thresholds differ across assays; compare carefully to patient labs.
