# Summary

- Developed and validated a multiplex droplet digital PCR assay to simultaneously detect Babesia, Bartonella, and Borrelia DNA across clinical blood, vectors, cell lines, and animal tissues.
- Assay detected 31 Bartonella, 13 Borrelia, and 24 Babesia species (plus some related piroplasms); no Treponema/Leptospira amplification.
- Analytical sensitivity ~0.2–5 genome equivalents/µL depending on species/matrix.
- Facilitated detection of multi-genus co-infections in clinical and experimental samples.

# Key data points

- Broad species inclusivity relevant to non-henselae Bartonella and Babesia co-testing.
- ddPCR may improve low-burden detection vs conventional PCR—context for PCR-negative commercial tests.
- Designed for co-infection questions common in Lyme-endemic specialty clinics.
- Does not define clinical specificity/positive predictive value in unselected patients.

# Caveats

- Assay development paper—not clinical diagnostic accuracy trial in this patient’s phenotype.
