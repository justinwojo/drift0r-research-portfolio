# Summary

- EAU Urolithiasis Guidelines Panel 2024 update dedicated to **metabolic evaluation** and pharmacologic/dietary recurrence prevention.
- Defines high-risk stone formers who warrant full metabolic workup and stone-specific algorithms (calcium oxalate, calcium phosphate, uric acid, cystine, infection stones).
- Standardizes blood and 24-h urine testing panels and follow-up intervals after preventive therapy.
- Current European counterpart to AUA 2014 medical management—use both when interpreting hypercalciuria in a bone-fragility context.

# Key data points

- Risk stratification drives intensity of evaluation; high-risk patients get full metabolic panels and often genetic consideration when indicated (e.g., cystinuria, primary hyperoxaluria).
- 24-h urine: calcium, oxalate, citrate, uric acid, sodium, volume, creatinine; interpret against method-specific reference ranges.
- Hypercalciuria interventions: fluid, sodium reduction, thiazides, alkali therapy when appropriate; evaluate systemic causes (HPT, sarcoid, RTA, vitamin D excess).
- Stone analysis remains foundational when any stone is available.
- Follow-up metabolic testing verifies biochemical response, not only clinical stone-free status.

# Caveats

- Stone-prevention focus; bone outcomes are secondary.
- Full text often paywalled; EAU full urolithiasis guideline PDFs are freely downloadable from uroweb.org.
- Confirm local lab reference ranges (mg/24h vs mmol/24h).
