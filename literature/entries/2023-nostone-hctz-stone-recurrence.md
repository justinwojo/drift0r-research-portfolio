# Summary

- Multicenter, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial (NOSTONE; NCT03057431) of hydrochlorothiazide (HCTZ) for prevention of kidney-stone recurrence.
- Patients with recurrent calcium kidney stones randomized to HCTZ 12.5, 25, or 50 mg once daily or placebo.
- Primary finding: incidence of stone recurrence did **not** differ substantially across HCTZ doses vs placebo over the trial period.
- Catalyzed re-evaluation of low-to-moderate once-daily HCTZ as automatic first-line pharmacologic stone prevention.

# Key data points

- Design: double-blind RCT; recurrent calcium stone formers; HCTZ 12.5 / 25 / 50 mg vs placebo once daily.
- Primary outcome: composite symptomatic or radiologic stone recurrence—no substantial difference among arms.
- Observed urine calcium reductions with these HCTZ regimens were modest in subsequent comparative discussions (order of ~40–50 mg/day cited in follow-on literature).
- Does not negate all thiazide data; challenges generalizability of older high-dose / different-agent trials and modern low-dose HCTZ practice.

# Caveats

- Recurrent-stone population; high baseline risk enrichment.
- Once-daily HCTZ only; does not test chlorthalidone, indapamide, or BID regimens, or thiazide + potassium citrate combinations optimized for calciuria.
- Secondary bone endpoints not the primary focus (see separate BMD analyses of NOSTONE data).
- Null stone result does not mean thiazides cannot lower urine calcium enough to matter for bone or for carefully selected patients.
