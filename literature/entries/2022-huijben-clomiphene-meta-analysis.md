# Summary

- Systematic review and meta-analysis of clomiphene citrate as off-label therapy for male hypogonadism to raise T while preserving intratesticular T and spermatogenesis (unlike exogenous TRT).
- Pooled evidence: significant increases in total T, free T, LH, FSH, SHBG, and estradiol; symptom scores (often ADAM) improve in many studies.
- Concludes CC is effective for biochemical and clinical hypogonadism symptoms; safety generally favorable but data limited.

# Key data points

- 19 studies included (4 RCTs, 15 observational); 1642 patients; MA on 1279.
- Therapy/follow-up duration 1.5–52 months.
- Pooled total T increase: **+2.60** (95% CI 1.82–3.38) — units as reported in MA (nmol/L-scale effect in source; interpret as large, clinically meaningful rise).
- Estradiol rises with T (aromatization / HPG stimulation) — relevant to AI co-therapy discussions.
- Side effects infrequently and inconsistently reported across studies.

# Caveats

- Heterogeneous quality; few high-quality long RCTs vs TRT.
- Off-label; not FDA-approved for male hypogonadism.
- Publication bias possible; adverse-event capture weak.
