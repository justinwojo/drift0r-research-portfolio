# Summary

- Practical Neurology infographic summarizing the first multidisciplinary UK consensus guidelines for adult idiopathic intracranial hypertension.
- Built from systematic review + specialist interest group PICO process + survey of clinicians who regularly manage IIH; reviewed by national specialty bodies.
- Three operational pillars: (1) investigation of papilledema and IIH diagnosis; (2) management strategies; (3) workup/management of acute headache exacerbation in established IIH.
- Anchors adult care pathways for someone with remote childhood IIH who needs contemporary standards if symptoms recur.

# Key data points

- Diagnosis remains a syndrome of raised ICP without mass lesion, typically with papilledema—secondary causes must be excluded.
- Management prioritizes vision protection and headache phenotype-specific care; weight management is central in typical adult IIH.
- Acute headache flares in known IIH need structured reassessment (not automatic shunt revision).
- Consensus document—not a new RCT.

# Caveats

- UK consensus 2018; US practice nuances may differ.
- Male / non-obese / remote childhood IIH patients are underrepresented in adult trial literature (IIHTT was mostly women).
- Research catalog only.
