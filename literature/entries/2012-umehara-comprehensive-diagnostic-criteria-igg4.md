# Summary

- Formalizes comprehensive diagnostic (CD) criteria for IgG4-RD for practical clinical use across organs.
- Three pillars: (1) clinical/radiologic organ swelling or mass; (2) serum IgG4 >135 mg/dL; (3) histopathology with IgG4+ plasma cells (and ratio criteria).
- Definite / probable / possible stratification depends on how many pillars are met.

# Key data points

- Serologic cutoff commonly cited: **serum IgG4 >135 mg/dL** (1.35 g/L).
- Histopathology: >10 IgG4+ cells/HPF and IgG4+/IgG+ plasma cell ratio >40% (context-dependent by organ in later refinements).
- Clinical examination must show characteristic diffuse/localized swelling or masses in one or more organs.
- Steroid responsiveness noted as supportive but not a sole criterion.

# Caveats

- Cutoff alone is nonspecific; many non-IgG4-RD conditions exceed 135 mg/dL.
- Superseded/refined by 2020 RCD criteria and 2019 ACR/EULAR classification.
- Not medical advice; research catalog only.
