# Summary

- Literature review written to educate spine surgeons on **bone health optimization (BHO)** before elective spine surgery.
- Osteoporosis is common, underdiagnosed, and linked to worse fusion/hardware outcomes.
- Summarizes screening (DXA, opportunistic CT HU, labs including vitamin D), secondary-cause awareness, and pharmacologic options (anabolics highlighted for severe disease / fusion biology).
- Explicitly supports delaying elective reconstruction when bone is inadequate—validates “fusion deferred until BMD improves.”

# Key data points

- Guidelines and pathway literature now support systematic BHO clinics, not ad hoc vitamin D alone.
- Anabolic agents (teriparatide/abaloparatide/romosozumab depending on region/label) increasingly discussed preoperatively for high-risk bone.
- Invalid lumbar DXA from degeneration remains a pitfall—use hip/radius or CT HU.
- Nutritional optimization (calcium/vitamin D protein) is necessary but not sufficient for T −4 disease.

# Caveats

- Narrative review, not a new RCT of BHO clinics vs usual care.
- Most surgical series older/female; young-male secondary OP requires parallel endocrine workup.
- Does not solve etiology (genetics, hypercalciuria, hypogonadism).
- Stone/hypercalciuria complicates anabolic choice—multidisciplinary planning required.
