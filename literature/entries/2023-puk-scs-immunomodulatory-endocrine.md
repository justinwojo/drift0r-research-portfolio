# Summary

- Systematic review (PRISMA) synthesizing **immunologic and endocrine** effects of deep brain stimulation (DBS) and spinal cord stimulation (SCS).
- Across included SCS studies, patterns generally show **decreased pro-inflammatory cytokines** (IL-6, IL-1β), **increased IL-10**, reduced VEGF, and increased neurotrophic factors (BDNF/GDNF)—i.e., net **anti-inflammatory / immunomodulatory** signatures rather than systemic inflammatory activation.
- DBS literature more mixed on IL-6 but animal data also favor reduced IL-1β/IL-6 and increased IL-10; endocrine notes include ACTH/corticosterone and oxytocin changes depending on target.
- **Important gap statement for the case:** the review does **not** establish a recognized syndrome of delayed multi-joint autoimmune flare as a standard SCS effect; literature leans opposite (anti-inflammatory).

# Key data points

- 33 references total: 20 DBS, 13 SCS.
- SCS cytokine pattern summarized as ↓IL-6, ↓IL-1β, ↑IL-10; ↓VEGF; ↑BDNF/GDNF (esp. burst stimulation contexts in some reports).
- Both animal and human models represented; heterogeneity of stimulation parameters high.
- Authors conclude neuromodulation can modify neurological disease trajectories via local immune modulation.
- No robust evidence package for SCS-triggered systemic autoimmunity analogous to drug-induced lupus or checkpoint flares.

# Similarity to Drift0r case (0–5)

**Score:** 4

**Why:**
- Directly addresses the hypothesis that SCS caused a systemic inflammatory/autoimmune multi-joint crash ~4 weeks after implant.
- Best available synthesis argues SCS more often **dampens** inflammatory cytokines—making primary device-driven autoimmune flare **less likely** than peri-procedural stress, infection, metabolic (e.g., thiamine) decompensation, or coincidental disease activity.
- Complements Vu 2024 biologic complications review (infection/hematoma taxonomy) already in corpus.

# Caveats

- Heterogeneous stimulation paradigms; small human cytokine series.
- Anti-inflammatory average effects do not prove impossibility of rare paradoxical reactions.
- Does not measure multi-joint clinical arthritis outcomes as primary endpoints.
- Full text access may be limited; abstract-level card.
- Not medical advice.
