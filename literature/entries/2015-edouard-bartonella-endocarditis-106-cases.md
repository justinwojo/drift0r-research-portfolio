# Summary

- Retrospective/prospective reference-center series of **106 Bartonella endocarditis** cases (2005–2013), diagnosed by modified Duke criteria plus microbiological methods.
- Demonstrates that *Bartonella* is a **major cause of blood culture–negative endocarditis (BCNE)** when systematically sought with serology and valve PCR.
- Provides practical performance data for IgG IFA cutoffs and molecular testing of excised valves.

# Key data points

- Diagnosis combined high-titer serology, PCR (blood/valve), histology, and culture when possible.
- Valve tissue PCR/histopathology is highly informative when surgery is performed.
- Species spectrum dominated by *B. quintana* and *B. henselae* in this European series.
- Illustrates the diagnostic algorithm used when routine blood cultures fail for an intracellular/fastidious pathogen.

# Caveats

- Endocarditis-only population.
- High-titer IgG paradigm does not transfer cleanly to chronic MSK suspicion without endovascular disease.
- Does not evaluate commercial FISH or immunoblot LDTs used in Lyme-specialty labs.
