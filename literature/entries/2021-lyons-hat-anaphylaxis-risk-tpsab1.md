# Summary

- Demonstrates that **HαT** (extra α-tryptase–encoding *TPSAB1* copies) is a **heritable risk modifier** for severe anaphylaxis, especially Hymenoptera venom and idiopathic anaphylaxis cohorts.
- HαT is enriched among patients with **systemic mastocytosis** and worsens anaphylaxis severity metrics in studied cohorts.
- Includes experimental support that mature tryptases can increase endothelial permeability (PAR-2–related biology).

# Key data points

- Tryptase genotyping by droplet digital PCR across SM, venom anaphylaxis, idiopathic anaphylaxis, and controls.
- HαT prevalence substantially higher in selected mast-cell–related clinical populations than general population (~5%).
- Elevated BST alone does not equal clonal disease; genotype clarifies risk stratification.

# Caveats

- Referral cohorts over-represent severe allergic phenotypes.
- Does not address osteoporosis or bone-marrow biopsy thresholds for HαT+ KIT− patients without anaphylaxis.
