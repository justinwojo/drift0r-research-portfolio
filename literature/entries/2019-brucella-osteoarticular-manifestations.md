# Summary

- Brucellosis is a zoonosis (*Brucella* spp.) from unpasteurized dairy or animal contact; fever, sweats, and musculoskeletal pain are typical.
- Osteoarticular disease is the most common complication (wide reported range ~10–85%): sacroiliitis, spondylitis, peripheral arthritis, bursitis, osteomyelitis.
- Can become chronic and destructive in the spine; mimics tuberculosis and pyogenic discitis.
- Diagnosis: blood cultures (special prolonged hold), serology (SAT/ELISA), PCR; MRI for spinal disease.

# Key data points

- Sacroiliitis is especially common in younger adults; spondylitis in older patients.
- Acute-phase reactants usually rise during active infection.
- Combination antibiotics for prolonged courses are standard of care in guidelines (ID specialist).
- Travel/occupation/food history is the pretest key in non-endemic areas (U.S. cases often foodborne or imported).
