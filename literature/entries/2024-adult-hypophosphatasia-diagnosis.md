# Summary

- Hypophosphatasia (HPP) is caused by loss-of-function *ALPL* variants reducing tissue-nonspecific alkaline phosphatase (TNSALP) activity.
- Adult HPP may present with recurrent fractures, pseudofractures, bone/muscle pain, dental loss, chondrocalcinosis, and low BMD that can be mislabeled osteoporosis.
- **Persistently low age-/sex-adjusted ALP is the laboratory cornerstone**; elevated substrates (PLP/B6, PEA, PPi) and genetics confirm.
- Enzyme replacement (asfotase alfa) is disease-modifying in selected phenotypes; bisphosphonates may be relatively contraindicated in true HPP.

# Key data points

- Adult diagnosis is easy to miss if ALP is not flagged as low (lab “normal” lower bounds vary; serial lows matter).
- True HPP with **repeatedly normal ALP is exceptional**—case reports of “normal ALP HPP” exist but are not the rule and demand genetics + substrates + clinical expertise.
- Low ALP also occurs with other states (antiresorptives, hypothyroidism, malnutrition, zinc/magnesium deficiency, massive transfusion, etc.)—not pathognomonic alone.
- Bone phenotype is more osteomalacia/impaired mineralization than pure high-turnover osteoporosis.

**Score:** 2

**Why:**
- **Matches (partial):** Young-adult severe low BMD, fragility, musculoskeletal pain, multi-year diagnostic odyssey—HPP is a classic “don’t miss” metabolic bone mimic.
- **Critical gate:** ALP value is decisive. If ALP has been **normal/high-normal** on multiple draws, classic HPP becomes unlikely. (Evidence pack does not list low ALP as a finding; clinicians should verify historical ALP explicitly.)
- **Mismatches even if ALP borderline:** Hypercalciuria with *SLC7A9*, thiamine-responsive neuropathy, hypogonadism, HαT, IgG4 pattern, imaging-neg polyarthralgia are not core HPP.
- **Actionable research note:** Chart-mine **all ALP values**; if any are low-normal/low, measure vitamin B6 (PLP) and consider *ALPL* sequencing—not because HPP is probable, but because it is treatably different from ordinary osteoporosis.

# Caveats

- Do not start empiric asfotase based on low BMD alone.
- Prior bisphosphonate exposure can lower ALP and confound interpretation.
- “Normal ALP HPP” literature is sparse and should not override serial ALP biochemistry without genetics.
