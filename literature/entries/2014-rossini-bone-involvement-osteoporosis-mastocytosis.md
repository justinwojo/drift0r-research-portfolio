# Summary

- Reviews the spectrum of skeletal disease in systemic mastocytosis: **osteoporosis (most prevalent)**, diffuse osteosclerosis, and focal lytic/sclerotic lesions.
- Highlights **high osteoporotic fracture risk**, particularly vertebral fractures and in men.
- Recommends routine **DXA and vertebral morphometry**; discusses complex bone-turnover biology and **bisphosphonates as first-line** for mastocytosis-related osteoporosis.

# Key data points

- Bone disease is common and clinically important even in indolent SM.
- Presentation can be dominated by fragility fractures with few classic mediator symptoms in some patients (theme of later case literature).
- Antiresorptive therapy is rational given osteoclast-predominant pathophysiology.

# Caveats

- Review focused on established mastocytosis, not HαT-only populations.
- Imaging/biopsy decisions still individualized.
