## 1. Executive consensus

- This is almost certainly a **multi-disease stack**, not one rare unifier. No single entity covers ≥6 of the 10 case anchors.
- **Architecture bucket: high.** Confirmed axes coexist: severe multi-site bone disease with ongoing same-scanner hip loss, historical profound hypogonadism, documented thiamine deficiency with repletion response, structural L5–S1 disease, HαT explaining basal tryptase, and a real IgG subclass/titer abnormality.
- **Module identity bucket: medium.** Which exact bone gene, whether Bartonella is present, whether SAD is formally confirmable, and whether residual small-fiber disease exists remain open.
- **Best single-primary alternative: monogenic / constitutional early-onset osteoporosis** (WNT1 / LRP5 / PLS3 / mild OI spectrum; HPP branch **low** unless age/sex-adjusted ALP is truly low). Expected panel yield is modest (~9% in a related young-adult idiopathic OP cohort), not “high-yield marketing.”
- **Infection is detachable, not foundational.** Active babesiosis is **low** and near-closed pending one properly instrumented test. Bartonella remains a **contributor** (minority: medium; majority lean low–medium) because specialty signals, periosteal/nodule clues, and a strong non-durable antimicrobial response exist without commercial confirmation.
- **HαT is not a disease driver.** It explains tryptase; it is procedural for SM (adjust tryptase criteria; modest enrichment in mastocytosis cohorts) and does **not** carry bone, joints, B1, or IgG1.
- **Hard rejects (3/3):** primary psychiatric/SSD, COVID-vaccine etiology, classic fibrotic IgG4-RD, Lyme, mold/CIRS as primary ongoing driver, classic MCAS / default KIT+ SM.

## 3. Working model cards (consensus)

### H1 — Layered stack (preferred)

**Mechanism sketch.** A constitutional or multi-hit skeletal problem (historical profound hypogonadism + persistent hypercalciuria/Na load ± monogenic fragility) produces extreme young-adult low BMD and interacts with structural L5–S1 disease. Independently, a qualitative humoral abnormality (low IgG1, non-protective Hib/pneumococcal titers) is real and may reduce NPV of some serologies. Independently, thiamine deficiency caused a documented dry-beriberi episode with stress-linked demand recurrence. Contested intracellular infection (Bartonella > Babesia) is an **optional** limb pending proper adjudication. HαT explains basal tryptase without carrying the case.

**Explains:** multi-system presentation without one specialty “home”; partial non-overlapping benefit from testosterone axis management, benfotiamine, and antimicrobials; normal CRP/ESR with severe pain; extreme OP + B1 + immune labs coexisting.

**Fails:** does not name a single cause; does not prove infection; does not identify the molecular bone lesion until genetics/formation markers return.

**Dangerous if missed:** yes — via progressive bone/spine risk and treatable modules (SAD, copper, infection if true, SM if true).

### H2 — Monogenic / constitutional early-onset OP (best single-primary)

**Explains:** L3 T −4.3, forearm T −3.3, ongoing hip −4.2%/yr same scanner despite years of T 500–800, atraumatic pars context, incomplete explanation by “historical low T alone.”

**Fails:** B1, Ig pattern, specialty infection signals, nodules/periosteal edema.

**Honest yield:** related early-onset idiopathic OP panels ~**9%** pathogenic rare-gene hit rate; high VUS rate; negative panel does not exclude unknown genes or non-genetic multi-hit secondary OP.

**Dangerous if missed:** yes — family counseling, surveillance, and **drug-class** decisions (HPP branch would contraindicate some antiresorptives if ever truly met).

### H3 — Current bone-loss physiology module

**Explains:** ongoing hypercalciuria (24h Ca up to 333 mg), high urine Na, upper-normal CTX without paired formation markers, continued hip loss.

**Does not claim:** that ALP 42–52 + CTX 616 already prove “uncoupled” disease — those values justify **measuring** P1NP/BSAP, not diagnosing from total ALP.

**SERM/estradiol:** not a swarm headline. Pack already records estradiol managed/in range later (T012). Survivable ask: retrieve actual E2 values + assay method (sensitive assay) for endo discussion only — **no drug-change recommendation**.

### H4 — Bartonella contributor (residual-primary)

**Explains best among infection hypotheses:** periosteal edema, imaging-negative nodules, specialty IgM trajectory, multi-domain antimicrobial response (non-durable).

**Does not explain:** magnitude of generalized OP, historical T ~34, hypercalciuria.

**Adjudication:** culture/PCR/sequence and tissue if safe — **not** more standalone IgM. BAPGM-type enrichment is a method paper, not validated clinical S/S for this phenotype; results remain specialty/reference evidence until independently confirmed.

**Author-ecosystem discount (chair note):** several pro-infection cards cluster in commercially interested groups (Galaxy / IGeneX ecosystems); independent seroprevalence work shows no reliable symptom signature. Lowers posterior for infection-forward stack branding.

### H5 — Humoral abnormality / possible SAD

**High confidence** that IgG1-low + non-protective titers are abnormal.  
**Low confidence** in the formal SAD label until same-lab, same-assay pre/post polysaccharide vaccine challenge + infection history (lit-0205/0206).
