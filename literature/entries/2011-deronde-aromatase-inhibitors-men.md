# Summary

- Review of aromatase inhibitor (AI) effects in males across short stature, delayed puberty, and adult hypogonadism contexts.
- AIs lower estradiol, raise LH/FSH and testosterone via reduced estrogen-negative feedback at hypothalamus/pituitary.
- Potential therapeutic niches, but **long-term efficacy and safety not established** — routine use not recommended by the authors.
- Frames estradiol as a physiologically necessary hormone in men (bone, fat, gonadotropin control), not merely a side-product to eliminate.

# Key data points

- Mechanism: block CYP19 aromatization of androgens → ↓E2 → ↑gonadotropins → ↑testicular T.
- Applications discussed: pubertal disorders, adult men with low T (off-label), obesity-related secondary HG concepts.
- Safety concerns include bone health, lipid changes, and unknown long-term outcomes.
- Open access PMC full text.
