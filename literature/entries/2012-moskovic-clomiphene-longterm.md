# Summary

- Long-term (>12 months) clomiphene citrate therapy in symptomatic hypogonadal men.
- Sustained elevation of total T over 1–3 years with improved ADAM scores and **improved bone densitometry** at femoral neck and lumbar spine.
- No adverse events reported in this cohort (n = 46).
- Positions CC as durable medical management, not only short fertility bridging.

# Key data points

- n = 46; mean age 44 years; baseline T 228 ng/dL.
- Follow-up T: 612 (1 yr), 562 (2 yr), 582 (3 yr) ng/dL (p < 0.001).
- ADAM score 7 → nadir 3 at 1 year.
- Femoral neck and lumbar spine BMD scores improved significantly.
- Also tracked estradiol and LH.

**Why:**

- Explicit bone densitometry improvement is highly relevant to markedly low BMD for age and the clinical goal of rebuilding bone.
- Age closer to patient’s young-adult onset than classic LOH trials.

# Caveats

- Uncontrolled; no placebo; BMD improvement may partly reflect other factors.
- “No adverse events” may under-ascertain psychiatric/visual/estrogenic effects known from broader CC experience.
- Does not study abrupt discontinuation crashes.
