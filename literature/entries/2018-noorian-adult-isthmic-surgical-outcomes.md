# Summary

- Systematic review of **clinical outcomes across surgical techniques** for **adult isthmic spondylolisthesis** (PLF, PLIF, TLIF, and related constructs).
- Core finding: multiple fusion strategies improve pain/function; **no consensus single best technique** across all outcome domains.
- Interbody methods often achieve higher radiographic fusion rates; clinical superiority is less consistent and technique-dependent.
- Reinforces that surgical goals are stabilization of the isthmic segment, neural decompression when needed, and durable fusion—not mandatory high-grade reduction for low-grade slips.

# Key data points

- Population: adult isthmic (not degenerative) spondylolisthesis surgical series.
- Outcomes pooled conceptually: ODI/VAS improvement, fusion rates, complications, reoperation.
- Reduction of low-grade slips is often unnecessary for clinical success (consistent with broader isthmic literature).
- Heterogeneity of study design, graft type, and instrumentation limits meta-analytic certainty.
- Informs pre-op counseling: expect meaningful average improvement, non-zero nonunion/revision risk.

# Caveats

- Not an RCT; quality of included studies variable.
- Does not stratify by markedly low BMD for age or young-male secondary bone loss—critical for this case.
- Sitting intolerance and multi-joint systemic disease outside scope.
- Not medical advice.
