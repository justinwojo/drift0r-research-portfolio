# Summary

- Establishes diagnostic criteria for IgG4-related ophthalmic disease (IgG4-ROD), integrating systemic IgG4-RD concepts with ocular adnexal presentations.
- Targets lacrimal gland, trigeminal nerve, extraocular muscle, and related orbital tissues previously labeled Mikulicz disease or idiopathic orbital inflammation.
- Combines imaging enlargement, histopathology (IgG4+ plasma cells ± fibrosis), and serum IgG4 >135 mg/dL.

# Key data points

- Imaging: enlargement of lacrimal gland, trigeminal nerve, or extraocular muscle, or mass/hypertrophic lesions in ophthalmic tissues.
- Histology: marked lymphocyte/plasmacyte infiltrate (± fibrosis, germinal centers); IgG4+/IgG+ ≥40% **or** >50 IgG4+ cells/HPF.
- Serology: serum IgG4 >135 mg/dL.
- Definite diagnosis typically needs imaging + histology + serology (or high-certainty combinations per scheme).
- Progressive mass-forming disease is the clinical paradigm—not fleeting idiopathic swelling.

# Caveats

- Criteria designed for persistent orbital inflammatory disease; poor fit for transient post-procedural or idiopathic swelling.
- Later revised (2023) to emphasize optic neuropathy risk.
- Research use only.
