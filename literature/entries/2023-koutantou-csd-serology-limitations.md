# Summary

- Literature review of serological diagnosis performance in typical CSD and recommended diagnostic pathway.
- Reported assay sensitivities ranged ~10–100% with more than half of studies <70% sensitivity and 71% of studies <80%; specificities 15–100% with many <90%.
- Cross-reactivity, variable antigen prep, cutoffs, and background seroprevalence undermine isolated serology.
- Authors conclude molecular assays (preferably on lymph node tissue when indicated) should be the confirmatory gold standard and encourage biopsy in suspicious cases.

# Key data points

- Sensitivity of serology often <70–80% across reviewed studies.
- Specificity frequently <90%; healthy population seroprevalence confounds interpretation.
- IgM and IgG IFA/ELISA ranges extremely wide (IgM sens ~20–90%; IgG ~26–100% in cited ranges).
- Diagnostic recommendation prioritizes PCR over serology alone for confirmation.

# Caveats

- Focus on typical CSD lymphadenopathy pathway; chronic bacteremia workups differ.
- Biopsy of nodes not applicable when no target node exists (Drift0r’s presentation).
- Does not evaluate IGeneX immunoblot validation specifically.
- Supports diagnostic humility, not automatic rejection of all infection hypotheses.
