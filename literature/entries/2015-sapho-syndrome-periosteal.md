# Summary

- SAPHO (synovitis, acne, pustulosis, hyperostosis, osteitis) spans sterile osteitis/hyperostosis ± neutrophilic skin disease; adult anterior chest wall disease is classic.
- Imaging: sclerosis, hyperostosis, **periosteal reaction**, mixed lytic-sclerotic lesions; MRI detects marrow edema/osteitis early.
- Skin disease (PPP, severe acne) is common but **not mandatory** in every definition set—isolated sterile osteitis exists.
- Labs may show elevated inflammatory markers during active osteitis, but some reported cases have normal CRP/ESR between flares or in limited disease.

# Key data points

- Hyperostosis and chronic periosteal reaction are highly characteristic on plain film/CT.
- Sternoclavicular, manubrial, and long-bone involvement patterns help differentiate from infection and malignancy.
- Overlap with spondyloarthritis features and CRMO continuum in younger patients.
- Treatment: NSAIDs, bisphosphonates, antibiotics (controversial), TNF inhibitors in refractory disease—clinical, not protocolized here.

# Similarity to Drift0r case (0–5)

**Score:** 2

**Why:**
- **Matches (partial):** Mild **humeral periosteal edema** on MRI is a thin SAPHO-adjacent hook; multi-year bone/joint pain; non-erosive peripheral joints possible.
- **Does not match well:** No reported palmoplantar pustulosis/acneiform driver; no florid hyperostosis on imaging summaries; generalized **polyarthralgia without osteitis map**; severe diffuse osteoporosis (not hyperostosis) is the opposite skeletal phenotype; normal CRP/ESR chronic course; thiamine/hypogonadism/HαT/IgG4 not explained.
- **Usefulness:** Keep SAPHO/CRMO on the imaging differential for unexplained periosteal change, but overall skeletal pattern (osteoporosis ≫ osteitis) argues against classic SAPHO as the unifier.

# Caveats

- Periosteal reaction is nonspecific (infection, stress, malignancy, venous stasis, HOA, etc.).
- Adult vs pediatric CRMO literature should not be conflated without care.
- Skin-negative osteitis requires careful exclusion of infection (including culture-negative osteomyelitis).
