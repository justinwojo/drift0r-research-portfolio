# Summary

- International ACR/EULAR classification criteria derived and validated in 1,879 subjects (cases + mimickers).
- Three-step process: (1) typical organ involvement entry; (2) **absolute exclusion criteria** (clinical, serologic, radiologic, pathologic); (3) weighted inclusion domains (clinical, serologic, radiologic, pathologic) with ≥20 points for classification.
- Excellent specificity (~97–99%) and good sensitivity (~82–86%)—designed for research classification, highly influential clinically.

# Key data points

- Must first demonstrate involvement of ≥1 of 11 typical organs in a manner consistent with IgG4-RD.
- Any of 32 exclusion items eliminates classification (e.g., highly specific autoantibodies, certain infections/malignancies, alternative histologies).
- Inclusion domains include serum IgG4 (higher multiples of ULN score more), typical radiology (e.g., bilateral lacrimal/parotid, retroperitoneal fibrosis pattern), and pathology (storiform fibrosis, obliterative phlebitis, IgG4 counts).
- Validation: specificity 99.2% / sensitivity 85.5% (cohort 1); 97.8% / 82.0% (cohort 2) at 20-point threshold.
- Mild isolated serologic elevation **without** typical organ entry does not classify as IgG4-RD.

# Caveats

- Classification ≠ diagnostic criteria for individual care, but widely used as a high-specificity framework.
- Dual publication (Arthritis Rheumatol PMID 31793250; Ann Rheum Dis PMID 31796497).
- Research catalog only.
