# Summary

- Evidence-based **IDSA clinical practice guideline** covering the spectrum of coccidioidomycosis from self-limited primary pneumonia to chronic pulmonary and **extrapulmonary disseminated** disease.
- Organizes actionable recommendations by syndrome and by special populations (immunocompromised, pregnancy, etc.).
- Emphasizes geography: residence or travel in the endemic Southwest (including parts of **Texas**, Arizona, California, etc.) is central to pretest probability.

# Key data points

- *Coccidioides immitis/posadasii* cause Valley fever; most infections are asymptomatic or mild CAP-like illness.
- Dissemination can involve skin, meninges, and **bone/joints**—skeletal disease may be chronic and destructive.
- Diagnosis: serology, culture, histopathology (spherules); bone disease may need biopsy.
- Antifungal therapy (azoles; amphotericin for severe disease) duration is often prolonged for disseminated osteoarticular infection—specialist-directed.

- 2016 document; local epidemiology and azole practice evolve.
