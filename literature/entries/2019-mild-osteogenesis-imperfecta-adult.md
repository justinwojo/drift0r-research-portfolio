# Summary

- Osteogenesis imperfecta (OI) is a genetically heterogeneous brittle-bone disorder; ~90% of classic cases involve dominant *COL1A1/*COL1A2* variants.
- Mild (type I) OI may escape childhood diagnosis and present in teens/adults as recurrent fractures, low BMD, bone pain, blue sclerae, hearing loss, or dentinogenesis imperfecta—or after a relative is diagnosed.
- Extraskeletal features vary; joint hypermobility and easy bruising can occur; height may be near normal in mild disease.
- Management centers on fracture prevention, rehabilitation, and in selected patients antiresorptive/anabolic bone drugs under specialist care.

# Key data points

- Mild OI is under-ascertained; population incidence figures underestimate non-deforming forms.
- Low BMD is nearly universal across OI severity; quantitative defects (null alleles) tend milder than structural glycine substitutions.
- Differential with idiopathic juvenile/early osteoporosis, HPP, and non-accidental injury (peds) is clinical + genetic.
- Genetic testing panels for early osteoporosis increasingly include OI genes.

**Why:**
- **Matches:** Extreme early osteoporosis (spine T as low as −4.3), joint fragility with trivial loads, possible connective-tissue vulnerability, L5 pars defects/spondylolisthesis can occur in collagen disorders, EDS reportedly “cleared” but OI is a different gene set.
- **Does not fully match as sole disease:** Migratory noninflammatory polyarthralgia, thiamine deficiency with dramatic response, idiopathic hypogonadism, HαT, IgG4↑/IgG1↓, contested vector-borne serologies, and low basal temperature are outside classic mild OI. No reported childhood fracture cascade or blue sclerae/hearing loss in the public pack.
- **Research value:** OI/genetic bone fragility panel remains a reasonable **bone-only** differential contributor even if it cannot explain the entire multi-system syndrome.

# Caveats

- Negative EDS evaluation does not exclude *COL1*-related OI.
- Family history may be absent (de novo variants).
- Genetic counseling recommended before testing.
