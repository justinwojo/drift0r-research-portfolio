# Summary

- Focused review of **bone phenotypes in systemic mastocytosis**, ranging from osteosclerosis to osteoporosis.
- Osteoporosis is among the most frequent and clinically relevant adult manifestations, often with **recurrent vertebral fragility fractures**.
- Mechanistic emphasis: **osteoclast-driven resorption** with relative/absolute uncoupling; **RANK–RANKL**, **histamine**, and other cytokines implicated.
- Bisphosphonates framed as the most rational first-line antiresorptive strategy for SM bone disease.

# Key data points

- Mast cell mediators and local marrow infiltration both contribute to skeletal disease.
- Fracture risk can be high even when densitometric osteoporosis thresholds are only modestly crossed (per broader SM bone literature).
- Treatment logic targets resorption excess.

# Caveats

- Not a controlled therapeutic trial.
- Does not evaluate HαT without clonality as a bone-loss disease.
