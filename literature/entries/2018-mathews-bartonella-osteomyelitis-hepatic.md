# Summary

- 12-year-old immunocompetent girl with B. henselae osteomyelitis involving bilateral iliac bones complicated by hepatic lesions.
- Authors note B. henselae is a rare osteomyelitis cause that should be considered when fever, pain, and lymphadenopathy fail routine osteomyelitis therapy.
- Example of multifocal bone + hepatic disseminated atypical CSD.

# Key data points

- Bilateral iliac osteomyelitis + hepatic disease in immunocompetent child.
- Failure to respond to routine osteomyelitis antibiotics prompted alternative pathogen consideration.
- Supports imaging + serology/PCR workup expansion in culture-negative bone infection.

# Caveats

- Pediatric case report.
- Disseminated febrile CSD phenotype differs from chronic noninflammatory polyarthralgia with normal CRP/ESR.
- Not periosteal-edema-only MRI correlate.
