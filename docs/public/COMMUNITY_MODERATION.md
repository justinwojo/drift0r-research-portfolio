# Community moderation and deletion policy

> **Research only — not medical advice.** Public GitHub issues are a gated research surface, not a clinic inbox.

## Principles

1. **Public means public.** Issues, comments, and most moderation actions leave a trail. Assume content can be indexed, archived, forked, or screenshotted.  
2. **Never paste private medical records** into public issues — even when requesting a correction. Quote only already-public site text when possible.  
3. **Blank issues are disabled.** Use structured templates only.  
4. **Speed with honesty.** Harmful content (records, doxxing, threats) is removed or locked quickly; research disagreements stay open when civil and sourced.

## What maintainers will do

| Situation | Typical response |
|-----------|------------------|
| Private records or non-public identifiers posted | Delete/edit content where the platform allows; close issue; remind of policy |
| Treatment instructions / personal medical advice requests | Close; point to licensed care and project scope |
| Harassment or brigading | Lock thread; restrict participants; report to GitHub if needed |
| On-topic literature or correction with weak citations | Ask for DOI/PMID; may decline if unfixable |
| Duplicate or off-template issues | Close with pointer to correct template |
| Privacy / removal request about published portfolio text | Track via template; correct or redact public materials when appropriate |

## Deletion limits (be realistic)

- GitHub may retain deleted content in logs, notifications, or backups.  
- Forks, clones, search-engine caches, and third-party archives **cannot be guaranteed erased**.  
- Unpublish of the site/repo (see private operator runbook) reduces official surfaces; it is **not** complete internet deletion.  
- Do not promise a requester that “everything is gone everywhere.”

## Specialty-lab and contested topics

Disputes about laboratory-developed tests (LDTs), MCAS criteria, infection serology, and similar topics are common and heated. Maintainers:

- Prefer linking to prepared FAQ and methods pages over re-litigating every thread  
- Require published sources for strong claims  
- May lock threads that become personal attacks or patient-advice sessions
