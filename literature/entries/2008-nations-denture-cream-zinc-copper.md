# Summary

- Case series demonstrating that chronic excessive use of zinc-containing denture cream can cause copper deficiency with profound neurologic injury.
- Patients presented with progressive myeloneuropathy and laboratory hypocupremia plus hyperzincemia; the zinc source was not dietary supplements but topical adhesive overuse (often with ill-fitting dentures).
- Removing the exposure and copper repletion are required; residual disability is common when diagnosis is delayed.
- Expands the exposure history beyond pills: ask about denture adhesives and other occult zinc sources.

# Key data points

- Mechanism shared with supplement zinc excess: metallothionein-mediated copper trapping in gut.
- Clue triad: neurologic SCD-like syndrome ± cytopenias + high zinc/low copper.
- Public health impact historically significant enough to drive product reformulation awareness.
- Differential still includes post-bariatric copper deficiency without zinc excess.
