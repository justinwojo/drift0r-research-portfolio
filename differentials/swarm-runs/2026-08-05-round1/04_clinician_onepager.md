## Case in one paragraph

Adult male with multi-year (~2017–) **non-inflammatory** migratory then generalized joint pain and fragility, **structurally confirmed** L5–S1 pars defects / listhesis (SCS implanted 2026), **extreme young-adult osteoporosis** (e.g. lumbar T-score to **−4.3**, forearm **−3.3**, same-scanner total-hip **−4.2% in 12 months**), historical total testosterone ~**34 ng/dL** later therapy-responsive, **documented thiamine deficiency** (serum **7 nmol/L**) with dry-beriberi features and dramatic repletion response, **hereditary α-tryptasemia** with basal tryptase ~14.5–17.7 (KIT method incompletely specified; urine mast-cell mediators normal), **IgG1 low / IgG4 elevated** with non-protective Hib and many pneumococcal titers, persistent **hypercalciuria** (24h urine Ca up to **333 mg**) with high urine sodium, and **specialty-lab** Babesia FISH / Bartonella IgM signals that remain **unconfirmed** by commercial/PCR pathways. Inflammatory markers repeatedly normal; broad seronegative CTD workup unrevealing. **Somatic symptom disorder formally reversed (2025)**; primary psychiatric framing is not appropriate as default.

## Top 10 diagnostic tests / workups (why)

1. **P1NP + bone-specific ALP** (± osteocalcin); ALP vs age/sex norms — formation side never documented; pairs with CTX before bone-agent decisions.  
2. **DXA add-ons:** Z-scores, vertebral fracture assessment (VFA), TBS; check hip change vs scanner **LSC**.  
3. **Controlled 24h urine** (Ca/Na/stone profile) + **fasting urine Ca/Cr** + paired Ca/PTH/PO4.  
4. **Early-onset osteoporosis gene panel** (honest ~single-digit–low-teens % yield in related cohorts; still decision-relevant if positive).  
5. **High-sensitivity peripheral-blood KIT D816V** (document method/LOD) before considering marrow given BMD risk.  
6. **Same-lab pre/post pneumococcal vaccine serotypes** (± Hib) + infection history → SAD yes/no.  
7. **Copper + ceruloplasmin + zinc** (escalate urine Cu only if indicated).  
8. **SFN/autonomic battery** (IENFD / QST / QSART ± tilt) — pain with normal EMG/imaging.  
9. **Infection split:** reference Babesia **smear + PCR during symptoms**; Bartonella culture/PCR/IFA (not IgM churn); tissue only if safe target. Specialty FISH/IgM stay **contested**.  
10. **Original HPG reconstruction** (at T~34 era) + **karyotype/CMA** when appropriate (pattern may lean central vs classic XXY).

## Don’t-miss bullets

- Progressive **fragility / silent vertebral fracture** risk with extreme BMD + pars disease.  
- **Monogenic bone disease** before committing long-term antiresorptive vs anabolic strategy.  
- **Assay-incomplete SM exclusion** — sensitive blood KIT first; marrow only if benefit > positioning risk.  
- **Copper deficiency** myeloneuropathy (can lack cytopenias).  
- **True antibody deficiency** if vaccine challenge fails.  
- **Endovascular/osseous infection** only if microbiologically confirmed — do not treat research notes as confirmation.  
- **Thiamine** status around any future major surgery (prior documented deficiency).  
- Local **spine/SCS red flags** (new weakness, bowel/bladder, saddle) — emergency pathway, not research delay.
