# Summary

- Narrative review arguing human bartonellosis may be under-recognized globally beyond classic cat-scratch disease.
- Summarizes mammalian reservoirs and potential arthropod vectors, noting Bartonella DNA detection across multiple vector taxa.
- Reviews expanding severe manifestations and the public-health implications of incomplete surveillance.
- Positions Bartonella as an emerging One Health pathogen with complex transmission ecology.

# Key data points

- Classic CSD is only part of the clinical iceberg; severe and atypical forms exist.
- Multiple reservoirs/vectors complicate exposure history interpretation (absence of known tick bite is not fully exclusionary).
- Calls for improved awareness and surveillance rather than providing new diagnostic cutoffs.
- Useful background for differential generation, not confirmatory evidence for an individual case.
