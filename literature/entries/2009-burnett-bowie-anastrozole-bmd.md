# Summary

- Double-blind RCT: anastrozole 1 mg daily vs placebo for 1 year in men ≥60 with low/borderline T and hypogonadal symptoms.
- Anastrozole increased testosterone and decreased estradiol as designed.
- **PA spine BMD decreased with anastrozole vs placebo** (statistically significant); other sites directionally similar.
- Conclusion: aromatase inhibition is **not** a bone-friendly strategy to raise T in aging men — skeletal estrogen is important.

# Key data points

- n = 69; anastrozole 1 mg daily.
- T: 319 → 524 ng/dL at 3 months (then ~474 at 1 year).
- E2: 15 → 12 pg/mL (stable thereafter).
- PA spine BMD: anastrozole 1.121 → 1.102 g/cm²; placebo 1.180 → 1.189 g/cm² (p = 0.0014 between groups).
- Bone turnover markers not significantly affected.

# Caveats

- Older men; daily dosing ≠ weekly men’s-health protocols.
- Not a study of AI + clomiphene combination.
- Does not prove patient’s bone loss was AI-driven (multi-hit etiology).
