# Summary

- Japanese MHLW team revision of 2011 comprehensive diagnostic criteria into **2020 RCD** criteria after worldwide uptake and recognized problems (biopsy access, IgG4 cutoff performance, immunostaining variability).
- Three domains: (1) clinical and radiological features; (2) serological diagnosis; (3) pathological diagnosis with explicit storiform fibrosis / obliterative phlebitis sub-items.
- Allows definite diagnosis when organ-specific criteria + robust pathology are met; retains serology as one domain, not the whole diagnosis.

# Key data points

- Domain 1: characteristic organ enlargement, mass, or nodular lesions on exam/imaging.
- Domain 2: elevated serum IgG4 (historical >135 mg/dL framework retained/updated in practice use).
- Domain 3: pathology including lymphoplasmacytic infiltrate with IgG4+ cells **plus** storiform fibrosis and/or obliterative phlebitis as key sub-items for higher certainty.
- Addresses false positives from isolated serology and false negatives when biopsy is difficult.
- Orbital disease explicitly within the multi-organ spectrum discussed.

# Caveats

- Diagnostic (Japan) vs classification (ACR/EULAR) systems differ in purpose and cut-points.
- Without biopsy, “definite” disease is hard to assign under RCD.
- Not medical advice.
