# Summary

- In 180 mastocytosis patients vs matched controls (plus validation and other myeloid neoplasm cohorts), **HαT was enriched in mastocytosis (17.2% vs 4.4%)**.
- HαT+ mastocytosis patients had **higher serum tryptase independent of mast-cell burden** and more severe mediator-related symptoms (including venom hypersensitivity/anaphylaxis associations in the study narrative).
- Establishes HαT as a **biomarker/modifier** within mastocytosis, not a substitute diagnosis that rules SM in or out.

# Key data points

- Digital PCR *TPSAB1* copy-number assessment.
- Median tryptase higher in HαT+ SM (e.g., ~49.6 vs ~34.5 ng/mL in the reported comparison)—still generally much higher than the patient’s mid-teens range.
- HαT enrichment appears relatively specific vs other myeloid neoplasms in this dataset.

# Similarity to Drift0r case (0–5)

**Score:** 3

**Why:** Critical for differential reasoning: HαT and SM can coexist; HαT inflates tryptase. Patient’s **tryptase 14.5–17.7**, **KIT negative**, and **no mediator phenotype** argue *against* typical SM+HαT presentations in this paper—but coexistence remains the “don’t miss” residual risk driving optional marrow discussion.

# Caveats

- Mastocytosis-ascertained cohorts; not generalizable to all HαT+ people in the community.
- Patient’s tryptase is far below medians of SM cohorts here.
- Blood KIT assays can miss low-burden disease; marrow remains gold standard when SM is strongly suspected for other reasons.
