# Summary

- Multicenter Italian dataset (**444** patients with mast-cell disorders) assessed germline *TPSAB1* copy-number gains by droplet digital PCR.
- Confirmed **HαT enrichment in mastocytosis (~13.3%)** vs general population (~5%).
- Analyzes clinical history, labs, and bone marrow findings to define how HαT modifies disease correlates (tryptase, mediator symptoms) within SM/related disorders.

# Key data points

- HαT is a common co-trait in SM clinics, not a rarity.
- Supports routine consideration of tryptase genotyping when interpreting basal tryptase and symptom burden in SM.
- Reinforces dual testing mindset: clonality workup (KIT/marrow) **and** HαT genotyping are complementary.

# Caveats
