# Summary

- Kindred with autosomal dominant **high bone density**, wide/deep mandible, and torus palatinus linked to chromosome 11q12–13 (*LRP5* locus).
- Identifies LRP5 G171V mutation segregating with the trait; absent in controls.
- Mechanism: impaired antagonism of LRP5 by Dickkopf-1 (Dkk-1) → unopposed Wnt signaling and increased bone formation.
- Completes the LRP5 allelic spectrum with Gong OPPG (loss-of-function / low mass).

# Key data points

- Affected subjects: high BMD phenotype without excess fractures; elevated osteocalcin/formation markers; normal resorption markers.
- Functional assays: LRP5V171 resists normal Dkk-1 inhibition of Wnt signaling.
- Conceptual bridge to anabolic strategies targeting sclerostin/DKK–LRP5 (later romosozumab).
- Demonstrates that LRP5 dosage/activity bidirectionally sets human bone mass.

# Similarity to Drift0r case (0–5)

**Score:** 1–2

**Why:**
- **Not a phenotypic match:** Patient has extreme **low** bone mass (T to −4.3), not high-bone-mass endosteal hyperostosis.
- **Conceptual match:** Same gene pathway explains why *LRP5* sequencing is informative for both high- and low-mass extremes; supports Wnt-pathway framing of monogenic OP differential.
- Useful when reading genetic reports that list *LRP5* variants of uncertain significance—direction of functional effect matters.

# Caveats

- High-bone-mass LRP5 is the opposite DXA phenotype; do not invoke this paper as an explanatory disease model for markedly low BMD for age.
- Different missense domains/effects (β-propeller vs LOF) → opposite clinical ends.
