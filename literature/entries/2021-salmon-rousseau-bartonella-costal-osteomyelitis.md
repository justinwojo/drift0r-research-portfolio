# Summary

- Second reported case of **unifocal thoracic/costal osteomyelitis** due to *B. henselae* in a child, expanding the anatomic map of Bartonella bone disease beyond vertebrae/pelvis.
- Reinforces that CSD-spectrum infection can present as a **focal bone lesion with soft-tissue reaction**, often culture-negative on standard media.
- Diagnosis relies on serology and/or molecular testing of involved tissue in the appropriate exposure context.

# Key data points

- *B. henselae* classically causes regional lymphadenitis; osteomyelitis is rare (~0.1–0.3% of CSD).
- Costal/ribs and other extraspinal sites are exceptional but documented.
- Imaging (MRI/CT) defines marrow edema, cortical change, and soft-tissue extension; histology may show granulomatous inflammation.
- Culture sensitivity is low; serology + tissue PCR are the practical confirmatory tools.

# Caveats

- Single pediatric case report.
- Does not establish chronic adult MSK bartonellosis as a syndrome.
- Specialty-lab IgM without tissue confirmation remains a different evidence grade.
